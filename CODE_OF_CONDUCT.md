# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, criticize ideas rather than people, protect private information, and avoid discriminatory or sexualized language or conduct. Project maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to [mail@benavo.ch](mailto:mail@benavo.ch). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Enforcement may range from a private correction through a permanent ban, based on severity and pattern of behavior.
