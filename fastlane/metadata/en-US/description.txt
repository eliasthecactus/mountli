MOUNTLI brings your existing cloud and server storage into Finder and Files using rclone and Apple’s File Provider framework.

Configure Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, S3, WebDAV, SFTP, SMB, Crypt, and every backend compiled into the included rclone release. Each mount appears as a normal Finder or Files location, while Apple manages local materialization.

Privacy is the product:
• No MOUNTLI account
• No hosted relay
• No analytics, ads, subscriptions, or paid tier
• Credentials encrypted on your device
• Direct connections to storage providers you choose
• Diagnostics are generated only when you request them

MOUNTLI is free, open source, and built natively for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.
